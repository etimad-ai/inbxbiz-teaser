01 — Logo

Lockup & usage

The mark keeps the supplied chevron geometry — a two-plane folded arrow that reads as "send" — in its three flat planes — navy body, teal upper fold, blue lower fold. It ships in exactly three finishes: full colour, all-navy, and reversed white. Clearspace equals the height of the arrow on all sides.

SUPPLIED ARTWORK
The current lockup as supplied. Its geometry is retained; the gradient is gone: Navy 900 body, Teal 500 #2FB495 upper plane, Signal Blue 600 #0B5CFF lower plane — readable at 16px and in one-colour print.

07 — Foundations

Spacing, motion, voice

SPACING — 4px base
4 · inline icon gaps
8 · label to field
16 · inside cards
24 · card padding
48 · section rhythm
MOTION
Fast and unshowy. Hover and focus 120ms ease-out; menus and drawers 200ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1); nothing longer than 240ms. Transition colour, opacity and transform only.
RADIUS
control 10 · card 14–18 · pill 999 · mark 16
VOICE
Plain, specific, unhurried. Name the object and the outcome: "Send for approval", not "Submit". Never exclamation marks, never "oops", no emoji in product copy.
Yes — "Maya needs to approve pricing before Friday."
No — "Uh oh! Something needs your attention 🎉"
